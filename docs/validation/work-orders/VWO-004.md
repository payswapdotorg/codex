# VWO-004 — Enterprise Operations User Validation

**Status:** MERGED
**Depends on:** VWO-001, VWO-002, VWO-003
**Wave:** 1
**Worker persona:** enterprise operations user

## Objective

Use the real application to create and exercise realistic employee workflows across construction, software/technology, ride-share, and media operations.

## Required teaching coverage

At least one complete workflow in each of DEMONSTRATE, INSTRUCT, HYBRID.

## Mandatory scenarios

Construction: daily site progress, procurement approval, safety incident.

Software: pull-request triage, production incident response, engineering onboarding.

Ride-share: driver onboarding, support escalation, surge operations.

Media: editorial publishing, content repurposing, breaking news.

## Acceptance

At least one meaningful workflow per industry reaches a real user-visible completion path. Across the worker's scenarios, Browser/Computer/Terminal/API/Tool/MCP/Human modalities are exercised where the workflow requires them. Failures and recovery are attempted. Product friction and missing surfaces are recorded, not worked around silently.

## Verification

Produce one consolidated report plus per-scenario evidence under `docs/validation/reports/` and `docs/validation/evidence/`, including exact runtime/repository/environment identities.
